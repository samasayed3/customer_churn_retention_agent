TeleNova Cancellation Policy

How to Cancel TeleNova Service
A TeleNova customer can request cancellation by phone, through a support agent, or in a TeleNova store. The customer must verify their identity before TeleNova processes the cancellation request. The TeleNova support agent explains the notice period, the fees, and the equipment return before confirming the cancellation.

Notice Period
TeleNova requires a notice period of 30 days for every contract type. The TeleNova service stays active and billed during the 30-day notice period. The cancellation date is the last day of the notice period.

Early Termination Fee for Month-to-month Contracts
A TeleNova customer with a Month-to-month contract pays no early termination fee. A Month-to-month customer can cancel at any time by giving 30 days notice.

Early Termination Fee for One Year Contracts
A TeleNova customer with a One year contract pays an early termination fee of 100 USD when the customer cancels before the contract end date. The early termination fee of 100 USD does not apply after the One year contract has ended.

Early Termination Fee for Two Year Contracts
A TeleNova customer with a Two year contract pays an early termination fee of 200 USD when the customer cancels before the contract end date. The early termination fee of 200 USD does not apply after the Two year contract has ended.

Equipment Return
Equipment provided by TeleNova, such as the modem, router, and set-top box, must be returned within 14 days after the cancellation date. Equipment that is not returned within 14 days is charged at its replacement value.

Final Bill
The final TeleNova bill includes the charges up to the cancellation date and any early termination fee. Any remaining balance is charged to the payment method on file.

Alternatives to Cancelling
Before processing a cancellation, TeleNova support agents tell the customer about relevant options. These options include an eligible retention offer, a plan change, or a contract change. The agent only presents offers whose eligibility conditions the customer meets. The customer may decline these options. If the customer declines, the cancellation continues without delay.

Retention Offers for Customers Thinking of Leaving
TeleNova has seven retention offers: Contract Upgrade Discount, AutoPay Switch Credit, Free Tech Support Trial, Fiber Price Lock, Loyalty Reward, Senior Care Package, and Welcome Bonus. A customer with a Month-to-month contract and a tenure of at least 6 months can be offered the Contract Upgrade Discount. A customer with a tenure of at least 49 months can be offered the Loyalty Reward. A customer who pays by Electronic check or Mailed check can be offered the AutoPay Switch Credit.

Cancelling While a Retention Offer Is Active
A TeleNova customer who cancels while a retention offer is active loses the remaining benefit of that offer. For example, a Loyal Customer who cancels after two months of the Loyalty Reward loses the remaining 4 months of the 20 USD monthly bill credit. A Fiber Plus customer who cancels during the Fiber Price Lock loses the price freeze.

Cancelling After the Contract Upgrade Discount
A TeleNova customer who accepted the Contract Upgrade Discount has a new One year or Two year contract. The early termination fee of the new contract applies if the customer cancels before the new contract end date. The fee is 100 USD for a One year contract and 200 USD for a Two year contract.

Cancelling the Premium Bundle
The Premium Bundle is sold only with One year and Two year contracts. A Premium Bundle customer who cancels before the contract end date pays the early termination fee of the contract. The services included in the Premium Bundle end on the cancellation date.

Reactivation After Cancellation
A customer who cancelled can return to TeleNova at any time. A returning customer is treated as a New Customer, with tenure starting again from zero. A returning customer must meet the eligibility conditions of an offer in the normal way.

Churn Risk
TeleNova predicts a churn probability for each customer. A probability below 0.3 is Low risk, from 0.3 to 0.6 is Medium risk, and 0.6 or higher is High risk. A customer with High risk is more likely to cancel.

Cancellation Cost Examples
A customer with a Two year contract who cancels with 10 months remaining pays 200 USD as the early termination fee, plus the bill for the 30-day notice period, and must return the equipment within 14 days. A customer with a Month-to-month contract who cancels pays only the bill for the 30-day notice period and returns the equipment within 14 days.

Frequently Asked Questions About Cancellation

Question: How much is the fee to cancel a One year contract early?
Answer: The TeleNova early termination fee for a One year contract is 100 USD.

Question: Is there a fee to cancel a Month-to-month contract?
Answer: No. A Month-to-month contract has no early termination fee, but 30 days notice is required.

Question: How long does a customer have to return the equipment?
Answer: The customer must return the equipment within 14 days after the cancellation date.

Question: How long is the notice period?
Answer: The notice period is 30 days for every TeleNova contract type.
